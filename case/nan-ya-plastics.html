CASE 02 · MARKETING & OPERATIONS

Marketing & Operations Specialist at Nan Ya Plastics.

EmployerNan Ya Plastics Corporation America
YearMay – Dec 2025
RoleMarketing & Operations Specialist
Sub-brandsNeuma Home · MP Doors · MySmartFit Chair

At Nan Ya Plastics Corporation America I led UX/design and brand execution across three consumer sub-brands: Neuma Home, MP Doors, and MySmartFit Chair, producing user-centered marketing collateral, product visuals, and brand-aligned digital experiences in Figma and Adobe Creative Suite. I refreshed website UX and social media presence across all three sub-brands, designed print and digital marketing materials including brochures, product sheets, and trade-show collateral, and built AI-assisted Excel reporting workflows that improved leadership report accuracy and reduced weekly prep time. Achieved zero missed compliance deadlines by maintaining audit-ready Asana documentation and risk-tracking dashboards across all project phases. The artifacts below are rebuilt as interactive demos.
